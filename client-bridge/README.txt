========================================================================
   JOURNEX PERSONAL MT5 CLIENT BRIDGE (OPTION A - BRING YOUR OWN PC)
========================================================================

WHY USE THIS BRIDGE?
- 100% Private: Your broker login & password never leave your computer.
- Direct Execution: Trades are placed locally on your MetaTrader 5 terminal.
- Zero Latency: Instant order routing to your broker (Exness, FTMO, IC Markets).

QUICK SETUP IN 3 STEPS:
1. Open your MetaTrader 5 desktop application and login to your account.
2. In MT5, ensure Algo Trading is enabled:
   Tools -> Options -> Expert Advisors -> Check "Allow algorithmic trading".
3. Double-click "start_bridge.bat".
4. Go to your Journex Dashboard:
   http://localhost:3000/accounts -> Click "Test Connection" -> Connected!

TROUBLESHOOTING:
- "Failed to initialize MT5": Make sure MetaTrader 5 desktop is open and logged in.
- "Module not found": Ensure Python is installed with "Add Python to PATH" checked.
